About Us

Your Trusted Aluminum Partner

Muta Glass & Aluminium has been serving the aluminium industry since 2021. We specialize in manufacturing, fabrication, and distribution of high quality aluminium windows, doors and gates.

Our workshop if equipped with cutting edge tools and materials to provide premium quality products for your home or workspace.

Our Products

Aluminum Windows

Custom aluminium windows available in various finishes.

We offer side hung, top hung and sliding windows with various glass options.

Aluminum Doors

We offer you standard, stable, pivot, folding and sliding doors in a variety of finishes.

Our doors are custom sized and we offer a variety of designs and glass options.

Aluminum Garage Doors

Whether you've got a single or double garage we've got you covered.

We have various designs and finishes and we also include a remote controlled motor.

Our Services

Glazing

We provide various glasses for your house, including replacement of broken glasses

We also do weatherproofing ans sealing for all types of windows and doors.

Installation & Maintainance

Installation of both windows, door and gates including garage motors.

Powder coating to restore the clour and enhance durability of aluminium windows and doors.
